Alma-Knack Marathon

This is a little game I have made to make me better at making drinks at work.

The set up involves placing a container of water in each relevant area (glass bottle with speed pourer in ice well 2, plastic squeezy bottle in ice well 1, speed pourer in fridge, bottle with lid on back bar) and getting some ice.

In order to pass each drink, you have to confidently pick up the relevant bottle, then place it back down, and pour the amount of water from its corresponding bottle into the relevant position. The drink must be made in its entirity, including each individial "ingredient", the relevant techniques, the correct glass, and "garnishes" can be included in the form of sticky notes.

Marathon

Make every drink on the menu as quickly as possible. Either plain mode, in which you go through them all, or simulation mode, in which you recieve extra challenges, such as distractions and group orders.
